\section{The argument, in seven steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item A COCO-trained detector applied to raw historical scans finds essentially nothing: $\text{mAP}_{50}=\num{0.0047}$.
  \item Training a student on the teacher's own pseudo-labels appears to fix this --- $\text{mAP}_{50}=\num{0.989}$.
  \item Graded against a \emph{human} instead of its teacher, the same model collapses to $\num{0.1462}$. The improvement was an illusion of self-agreement.
  \item Disagreement between four heterogeneous models predicts real errors well on ambiguous, scene-positive content: $\text{ROC-AUC}=\num{0.9724}$ ($C_1$, $n=801$).
  \item It ties the best single-model baseline on discrimination ($0.9724$ vs $0.9726$) but wins on \emph{calibration}: $\text{ECE}<\num{0.001}$ vs $0.19$.
  \item It fails where all four models share the same systematic error --- on confirmed true negatives, $\text{ROC-AUC}=\num{0.1259}$, well below chance.
  \item \textbf{The contribution is therefore not ``AI replaces archivists''} but a routing mechanism that measures where automated analysis should and should not be trusted.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The data-integrity correction (asked about most)}
An earlier version of this thesis reported the triage guarantee as \emph{failing} out-of-sample. Investigating why found the cause was the evaluation pool, not the method: \num{1{,}199} of its \num{2{,}000} images carried unverified, catalog-default ``no scene'' labels that had never been through human review.

Three independent raters have re-checked \num{734} of them and found \num{57.5\%} mislabeled --- real scenes marked absent. Rebuilt on a corrected \num{1{,}250}-image pool (\num{196} confirmed negatives), the guarantee \emph{holds}. The lift estimate was itself revised down twice as the negative sample grew from $7$ to $38$ confirmed images ($3.62$--$7.10\times \rightarrow 2.31$--$3.09\times$) --- reported openly, because a negative-control statistic should not be trusted before its sample is large.

\section{What this thesis does \emph{not} claim}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Not a window into what a model ``knows.''} $U_{\text{SAA}}$ is a proxy built from disagreement, not a measurement of epistemic state.
  \item \textbf{Not the only option.} A single model, asked correctly, reaches $0.9726$. The contribution is calibration, not exclusivity.
  \item \textbf{Not deployment-general.} A conformal guarantee is valid only relative to the distribution it was calibrated on.
  \item \textbf{Not proven to transfer.} See below --- it demonstrably does not, without recalibration.
  \item \textbf{Shapley explains, it does not prove causation.}
\end{itemize}

\section{Two honest negative results}
\textbf{0. Nine formulations, none better than plain confidence.} The uncertainty score does not outperform a single model's confidence, and this is not for want of trying a better estimator: nine formulations were evaluated against a non-circular label --- four signal sets, three weighting schemes, three operators including the mutual-information (BALD) decomposition. The best reaches \num{0.7650} against \num{0.8167} for confidence. Removing the weakest member makes the spread \emph{worse}, and so does competence weighting, which rules out pruning and reweighting. The limit is the ensemble, not the formula: all four members are web-pretrained VLMs and fail together on the confirmed negatives.

\textbf{1. The shared blind spot.} On confirmed true negatives all four models confidently agree on the same mistake, so disagreement cannot see it ($\text{ROC-AUC}=0.1259$, $n=319$, \num{50} errors, $95\%$ CI $[0.062,0.196]$). A third annotation pass replicated this on \num{123} negatives disjoint from the original estimate ($0.1852$), so it no longer rests on a small sample. This is the method's boundary condition, and it is the reason the thesis frames its contribution as routing rather than replacement.

\medskip
\textbf{2. Transfer to a new archive fails --- and the reason is instructive.} Ground truth was collected for \num{23} of \num{24} images downloaded live from the Europeana API. All are human-confirmed scene-positive; the unchanged pipeline misses \num{65.2\%} of them. The error-\emph{detection} question could not be scored at all, because the ensemble collapsed. The intuitive symptoms mislead here --- the VLM is two-valued on Europeana \emph{and} on Colibri, and fusion is object-dominated ($r\approx0.98$--$0.99$) on both by construction. What separates them is variance: measured by the effective rank of the agent covariance spectrum, Europeana carries \num{1.37} independent signals (CI \num{1.06}--\num{1.61}) against \num{2.50} on Colibri. \textbf{A disagreement-based method requires the ensemble to stay heterogeneous on the target domain} --- and that is checkable from agent scores alone, before any labelling.

\section{Reproducibility}
\begin{itemize}
  \item One command regenerates every cited number from cached model outputs --- no GPU, no paid API calls, about a minute: \texttt{bash scratch/run\_full\_master\_experiments.sh}
  \item Last full verification: \num{159} of \num{161} result artifacts reproduced \emph{byte-identically}; the two exceptions differ only in the final floating-point digit and are cited nowhere.
  \item \num{17} of \num{17} cited headline numbers matched the freshly regenerated artifacts exactly.
  \item Key scripts carry a \texttt{-{}-verify} flag that recomputes and fails loudly if an artifact has drifted.
  \item Full instructions, plus a claim\,$\rightarrow$\,script\,$\rightarrow$\,artifact table: \texttt{REPRODUCE.md}
\end{itemize}

\section{Known limitations, stated plainly}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \num{465} of the \num{1{,}199}-image default-labelled block remain unreviewed; completing them would tighten the negative-control estimate further.
  \item The Europeana pilot is $n{=}24$ and contains no negative controls.
  \item Retrieval relevance labels are a proxy that shares agent scores with the retrieval signal --- supplementary evidence, not an unbiased benchmark.
  \item Validated empirically on one archive. Architecturally general in principle; not demonstrated across domains.
\end{itemize}
