Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

This slide shows every number for the example. On the left, the eight object classes. The object finder boxes both the boy and the woman as persons, but neither as a child, and gives the hen almost nothing. The describer is sure about both the person and the child, and gives the hen 0.311. After mapping, the agents’ probabilities are averaged — the scene matcher also contributes here — giving 0.93 for person, 0.37 for child and 0.23 for animal. The list threshold at a 10 percent promise is 0.088, so the list is person, child and animal. The annotator drew boxes for the woman and the boy only; the animal is the hen, which is really in the picture. On the right, the six scene answers. The scene matcher likes family best; the describer says yes to drawing, and half yes to family and teaching. The single learned mapping of all 27 numbers puts family first at 0.54 and drawing second — and family is the human label. With the old fixed weights the answer would have been drawing.
