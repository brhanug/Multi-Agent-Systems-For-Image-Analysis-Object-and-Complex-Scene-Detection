This is Figure 6.1 of the thesis, the corrected design as drawn there. It is the same structure as the six-step slide: three agents that never see each other, each report mapped to probabilities, three answers, and conformal risk control deciding what is approved and what a person checks. The text reader on the right takes no part in the decision; its output goes only to the Tier 2 helpers at the bottom, which run beside the decision on every picture.
