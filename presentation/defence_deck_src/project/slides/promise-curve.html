Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

Here is the same procedure as a picture, on the 252 calibration pictures of the fixed split. The horizontal axis is the cut-off lambda: a picture is approved when its uncertainty is below lambda. On the left is the share of calibration pictures that would be approved and wrong. It starts at zero and can only rise: every step up is one more wrong picture slipping through. The dashed line is the limit at five percent, after the small allowance: at most 11 of the 252. The cut-off is the last point before the curve crosses that line: lambda hat equals 0.829. On the right is what that buys: 70.6 percent of the calibration pictures are approved, and on the test pictures 70.7 percent. Nobody chose 0.829 by hand; it falls out of the labelled examples and the promise.
