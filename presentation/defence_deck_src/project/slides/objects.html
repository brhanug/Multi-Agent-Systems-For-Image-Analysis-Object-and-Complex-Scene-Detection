Part 4 of 5 · Results

Third result: the eight object classes, on the 300 pictures where I drew boxes by hand. The system ranks pictures that contain a class above those that do not with a mean AUROC of 0.903 — good. The risk promise here is different: at most ten percent of a picture’s objects may be missing from its list. That gives a list of 2.8 classes per picture, of which on average 1.56 are wrong and a person strikes them out. Careful wording: the list misses 9.2 percent of a picture’s objects on average, which is what is promised; counted over all objects it is 10.8 percent, because pictures without objects cannot miss anything. Automatic confirmation of single objects is not possible at a useful level, and the boxes are still weak for rare classes.
