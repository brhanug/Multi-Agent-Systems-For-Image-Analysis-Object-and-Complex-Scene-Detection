Objects

Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

Step four: three answers, and each combines the agents in the way that worked best when tested. Scene at all: a weighted average of the three probabilities, 0.6, 0.2, 0.2; scene if it reaches 0.5. I tested learned weights here and they give exactly the same result, so the weights do not matter at this level. Scene class: here the fixed weights were bad, so the system learns one mapping from all 27 numbers straight to the six answers, and picks the most probable; this raised balanced accuracy from 0.401 to 0.544. Objects: for each class, the average of the three agents’ probabilities; classes above a threshold go on a list. For the example: 0.97, a scene; its class is “family” at 0.54 — the old fixed weights would have said drawing; and its objects are person at 0.93, child at 0.37 and animal at 0.23.
