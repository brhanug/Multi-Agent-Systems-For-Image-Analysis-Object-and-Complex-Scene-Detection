Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

This is the corrected design, the system the thesis proposes. It has six steps, and the next slides take them one at a time. One: the scan is restored once. Two: three agents look at it independently — the object finder gives eight class scores plus boxes, the scene matcher gives five scene scores and one “no scene” score, and the describer gives thirteen yes-strengths. They never see each other’s output. Three: each report is turned into probabilities by a small learned mapping. Four and five: the probabilities are combined into three answers — which objects, which scene class, and is there a scene at all — and each answer gets an uncertainty. Six: conformal risk control decides whether to approve, to give a person a short list, or to send the picture to a person. The differences from the old design: real measurements, no derived Agreement term, and confidence instead of disagreement.
