Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

Here is the real picture we have been following: an old woman at her spindle, talking to a boy on a stool — and if you look closely, a small hen under the bench. It is a test picture, so the system never saw it during training or calibration. Each agent misses something. The object finder finds both people, but calls neither of them a child and gives the hen only 0.013. The scene matcher likes the family sentence best. The describer sees the boy and partly the hen, but for the scene it says yes to drawing, family and teaching all at once. Mapped to “scene” probabilities: 0.98, 0.94 and 0.99; combined, 0.97 — clearly a scene, so it is approved. The class mapping, which weighs all 27 numbers, puts family first at 0.54 — correct, while the old fixed weights would have said drawing. But 0.54 is not sure enough for the five percent promise, so a person confirms the class. The object list is person, child and animal. The annotator drew boxes only for the woman and the boy — the animal on the list is the hen, which is really there.
