Part 1 of 5 · The question

The talk has five parts and tells one story. First, the question. Second, the design as it was first built: why I chose several agents, how it computed its answers, and what broke. Third, the corrected design: I turn every problem into a design choice, then walk through its six steps with one picture. Fourth, the results — inside the labelled pictures, against baselines, and on the real archive. Fifth, what it all means.
