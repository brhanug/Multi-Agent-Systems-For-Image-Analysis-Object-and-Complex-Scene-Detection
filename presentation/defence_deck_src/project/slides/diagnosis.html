Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

This slide is the bridge between the two designs. On the left, every problem the audit found; on the right, what the corrected design does about it. The four signals came from one model and gave only eight states — so now three agents each report their own measurement, 27 numbers. The Agreement term was computed, not measured — it is gone. The scene sentences were a forced choice that could never say “no scene” — now each sentence is scored on its own, plus a “no scene” score. The teacher had blind spots — a better teacher, and everything graded against people. Disagreement did not warn of errors — so the system routes by its own confidence. Weights were set by hand — now they are learned. And the promise failed on the archive — so it is calibrated on a random sample of the archive. Nothing in the corrected design is there by habit; each part answers a problem.
