Master’s thesis defence · University of Hildesheim

Multi-agent systems for image analysis: object and complex scene detection

When can an automatic answer about a historical illustration be trusted without a person checking it?

Brhanu Atsbaha

First advisor: Prof. Dr. Thomas Mandl · Second advisor: Shereen Elsayed

Good morning. My thesis asks one practical question: a computer system labels thousands of old book illustrations — when can we trust its answer without a person looking? I will show the system as it was first built, why its central idea failed, the corrected design that replaces it, and how the corrected design does on the real archive.
