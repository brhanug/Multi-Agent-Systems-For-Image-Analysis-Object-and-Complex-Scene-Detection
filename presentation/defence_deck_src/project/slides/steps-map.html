Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

Before the details, here is the map. Each row is one step: what goes in, what it does, what comes out. One: the raw scan is restored once. Two: three agents look at it independently and give 27 numbers. Three: each agent’s numbers are mapped to probabilities by a small learned regression. Four: the probabilities are combined into the three answers. Five: each answer gets an uncertainty. Six: conformal risk control compares the uncertainty with a cut-off and decides — approve, give a short list, or send to a person.
