Part 3 of 5 · The corrected design

Step five: how sure is each answer? For “scene at all”, the probability p sits between 0 and 1, and 0.5 is the line between scene and no scene. A picture near the line is unsure, so the uncertainty is one minus the distance from 0.5. The example has p = 0.97, far from the line, so its uncertainty is low, 0.53 on this scale. For the scene class, the uncertainty is one minus the largest of the six probabilities; for the example, 1 − 0.54 = 0.46. For objects, each class simply keeps its probability, and a list is made of the classes above a threshold. Low uncertainty makes a picture a candidate for approval, but how low is low enough is decided in step six, not by me.
