The two tasks (fixed at the start)

What the system has to say about every picture

Task 1 · Which scene?

One answer out of six:

teaching · family · playing · landscape · drawing — or no scene

“Drawing” = figurative content that fits none of the other four.

Task 2 · Which objects?

Any of eight classes, several at once:

person · child · animal · building · weapon · vehicle · tree · text

The object finder also says where (boxes).

First level of Task 1: “scene at all?” — yes or no. It has the most labels, so the risk promise and the archive tests are measured on it first.

Part 1 of 5 · The question

There are two tasks, and their class lists were fixed at the start and never changed. Task one: which scene does the picture show — five classes, or no scene at all. “Drawing” is the catch-all class for figurative pictures that are not teaching, family, playing or landscape. Task two: which of eight object classes appear; a picture can contain several. The yes/no question “is there a scene at all” is the first level of task one. It matters because it has the most labels, so it is where I test the risk promise most carefully.
