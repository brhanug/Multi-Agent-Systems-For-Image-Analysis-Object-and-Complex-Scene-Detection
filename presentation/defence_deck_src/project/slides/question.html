Models can label every picture. The hard part is knowing which labels a person must still check — and promising how many wrong labels slip through.

illustrations from historical books (Colibri archive)

The archive has 12,110 illustrations. Nobody can describe all of them by hand. Modern models can produce a label for every picture, but they make mistakes, especially on old prints. So the real question is not “can a model label a picture” but “which of its labels can we accept without checking, and how many wrong ones will slip through”. That is what this thesis measures.
