Part 4 of 5 · Results

Here the system is applied to every picture in the archive, so you can see that it really classifies scenes and objects. Of the 10,860 pictures outside the labelled pool, about half are called “drawing”, a third “no scene”, and a few percent each family, landscape and playing. A person is found in 56 percent of pictures, an animal in 24. This also exposed an inconsistency: with the old labels, the yes/no level and the class level disagreed on a third of the archive; trained under one definition of a scene, they agree on 97 percent. I make no accuracy claim here — the scene classes of random archive pictures are being labelled now for exactly that test.
