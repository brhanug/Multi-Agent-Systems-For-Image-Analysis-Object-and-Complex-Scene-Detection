Part 5 of 5 · What it means

The honest limits. The biggest one: the archive labels come from one person, me; a second rater on the 400 random pictures would make the archive result much stronger, and it is low effort. Second, the scene classes do not carry over to the archive: on random archive pictures most scenes are children playing, and the mapping learned on the pool falls from 0.544 to 0.351 balanced accuracy; a mapping learned on archive pictures would be needed. Third, the rare classes have few examples, teaching only six. Fourth, the promise holds on average, not for every batch; I tested the standard remedy, Learn then Test, which reduces batches over the level from 41.5 to 17.5 percent at a small cost in approvals. And everything is on one archive. Tier 3 was designed but not run.
