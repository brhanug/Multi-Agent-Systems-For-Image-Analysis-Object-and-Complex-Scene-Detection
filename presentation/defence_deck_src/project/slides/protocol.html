How was all this tested? There are 1,250 labelled pictures from 550 books for the scene/no-scene level, 992 of them with a known scene class, 300 pictures where I drew object boxes by hand, and two samples drawn at random from the archive, 400 and 300 pictures. The books are split into training, calibration and test books, 60/20/20, so no book appears on two sides, and this split is redrawn 200 times; every figure I show is a mean over those 200. I call a difference “established” only if its whole 95 percent range is on one side of zero — that is stricter than the standard corrected t-test. The key tests were written down before they were run, and an error is always defined by a person’s label, never by the agents themselves.
