Part 2 of 5 · The first design and why it failed

This is Figure 2.1 of the thesis: the system as it was first built and run over the whole archive. Read it from top to bottom. Every scan is restored once. Four models look at it; the text reader is only used for analyses. Their outputs become numbers, and two formulas use them. On the left, SAA: a weighted average that decides “scene or not”. On the right, U-SAA: the spread of the numbers — how much the models disagree. The routing score mixes that spread with the average confidence; if it is below a cut-off chosen by conformal risk control, the picture is approved, otherwise a person reviews it. Tier 3 would have come before the person, but was not run. Tier 2 helpers run beside this on every picture and never change a decision. The key idea of this design: disagreement means doubt.
