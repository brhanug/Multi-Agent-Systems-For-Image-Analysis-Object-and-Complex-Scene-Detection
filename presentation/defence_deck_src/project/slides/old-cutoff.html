Part 2 of 5 · The first design and why it failed

Where does the cut-off 0.372 come from? Because every picture sits in one of eight rows, the routing score U-triage also has only eight values — the table lists them from lowest to highest. The cut-off is chosen by Algorithm 3, which I explain fully later: it tries cut-offs 0.001, 0.002 and so on, on the 252 calibration pictures, and keeps the largest one that keeps the five percent promise. Moving the cut-off only changes anything when it passes one of these eight values. Up to 0.3726 is safe. Passing it would approve the highlighted row — 2,217 pictures whose SAA sits exactly on the 0.5 line, and whose errors would break the promise. So the answer is 0.372. When I resampled the calibration pictures 2,000 times, it came out 0.372 every time. Across the whole archive that approves 8,490 pictures, and 3,620 go to a person — most of them from that one row.
